About the System

What is RTMS?

The Road Traffic Management System (RTMS) is a fully integrated, AI-assisted traffic enforcement and fine management platform built specifically for the United Republic of Tanzania. It combines fixed ALPR (Automatic License Plate Recognition) cameras, a real-time officer dispatch system, a citizen payment portal, and government regulatory integrations into a single, seamless platform.

RTMS is designed to modernise how Tanzania enforces road traffic laws — moving from manual, paper-based fine issuance to a fully digital, auditable, and transparent system that serves citizens, officers, cashiers, and government agencies simultaneously.

The system supports Mobile Money payments (M-Pesa, Airtel Money, Tigo Pesa) via Azampay, SMS notifications via Notify Africa, and deep integration with LATRA, TIRA, and TRA for real-time commercial vehicle compliance checking.

Automated Detection
AI-powered ALPR cameras scan all lanes simultaneously — detecting both front and rear plates, identifying violations in milliseconds, and issuing fines automatically without human intervention.

01 Overview & Scope

The Road Traffic Management System (RTMS) processes personal data in accordance with the Tanzania Personal Data Protection Act, Cap. 447 (PDPA 2022). This policy applies to all data subjects whose information is captured, stored, or processed by RTMS — including vehicle owners, traffic offenders, registered portal users, and law enforcement officers.

Data is processed on servers hosted in the United States (Render Infrastructure, Ohio) under a Data Processing Agreement requiring full compliance with Tanzanian law.

RTMS does not collect facial biometric data. All facial features captured in vehicle images are automatically pixelated before storage (PDPA 2022 compliance).

03 Legal Basis for Processing

Traffic enforcement is a statutory function under the Road Traffic Act, Cap 168. RTMS processes vehicle detection data under public interest and legal obligation. When citizens register on the portal, processing is based on freely given consent.

04 Face & Biometric Anonymization

Every vehicle image is automatically processed with a pixelation mosaic over the driver's face area before storage or transmission. This is applied at the point of capture and cannot be reversed. A green "ANON" badge confirms anonymization was applied to each image.

Face anonymization may only be disabled by a senior system administrator under a valid court order. All such events are audit-logged.

05 Data Retention

  • Detection images (compliant vehicles): 21 days, then auto-deleted
  • Violation evidence images: retained until fine is resolved, then 90 days
  • Fine & payment records: 7 years (TRA audit requirement)
  • User account data: until account deleted + 30 days
  • Officer activity logs: 2 years

06 Your Rights

Under PDPA 2022 you have the right to: access your data, request correction, request deletion, object to processing, and receive your data in a portable format. Submit requests to privacy@rtms.go.tz. We respond within 30 days.

Regulatory Compliance

LATRA Compliance

The Land Transport Regulatory Authority (LATRA) regulates commercial and public service transport vehicles in Tanzania under the Land Transport Regulatory Authority Act, CAP 413.

🚛
Vehicles Subject to LATRA
  • Intercity and long-distance buses
  • Daladala (commuter/minibus)
  • Trucks, lorries and freight carriers
  • Container and tanker vehicles
  • Online taxis and special hire
  • Government commercial transport
✅
Vehicles Exempt from LATRA
  • Private/personal passenger cars
  • Privately owned motorcycles
  • Non-commercial pick-up trucks
  • Private SUVs and saloon cars
Note: TIRA insurance checks still apply to ALL vehicles including those exempt from LATRA.
LATRA Violation Types detected by RTMS: No Transit Permit (TZS 500,000) · Expired Permit (TZS 300,000) · VTS Device Tampering (TZS 1,000,000) · Route Violation (TZS 400,000) · E-Ticketing Non-Compliance (TZS 200,000)
Regulatory Compliance

TIRA Compliance

The Tanzania Insurance Regulatory Authority (TIRA) mandates that every vehicle operating on Tanzanian roads holds a valid third-party insurance policy. RTMS checks insurance status for every vehicle detected by any camera — private or commercial.

Uninsured vehicles trigger an immediate officer alert regardless of vehicle category. Fines for no insurance: TZS 250,000. Expired insurance: TZS 150,000.

TIRA checks apply to ALL vehicles: private cars, motorcycles, trucks, buses, taxis, and government vehicles. No vehicle category is exempt from mandatory insurance requirements.

Surveillance Policy

The RTMS Surveillance module operates exclusively within the legal framework of the United Republic of Tanzania and is restricted to authorised law enforcement personnel (ADMIN and OFFICER roles only).

What the surveillance module does:

  • Flags vehicles with an active warrant, court order, or stolen status on a watchlist
  • Alerts officers when a watched plate is detected by any camera
  • Links National ID numbers to registered vehicles for authorised police lookups
  • Logs security incidents at camera locations with GPS coordinates and evidence

What the surveillance module does NOT do:

  • Facial recognition — no biometric identification of individuals
  • Mass surveillance — only flagged/watched plates trigger alerts
  • Sharing data with unauthorised third parties
  • Processing sensitive personal data without legal authority
All NID lookups are audit-logged with the officer's identity, timestamp and justification. Access requires a valid warrant or police case reference. Unauthorised use is a criminal offence under the PDPA 2022.

Source: Kenya e-fine collection data, comparable mobile money markets

TZS 20,000–300,000 depending on violation type
Source: Tanzania Road Traffic Act Cap 168, RTMS fine schedule

8–15% of collected fines, standard for PPP traffic enforcement globally
Source: South Africa AARTO model, Kenya NTSA-Safaricom agreement

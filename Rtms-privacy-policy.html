Section 01

Overview & Scope

The Road Traffic Management System (RTMS) is an automated traffic enforcement and management platform operated in the United Republic of Tanzania. RTMS uses fixed Automatic License Plate Recognition (ALPR) cameras, a central processing database, a web-based officer dashboard, and a public-facing citizen portal to detect traffic violations, issue fines, and facilitate payment.

This Privacy Policy applies to all data subjects whose information is collected, processed or stored by RTMS, including vehicle owners, traffic offenders, registered portal users, law enforcement officers, and any person whose vehicle is captured by an RTMS camera.

Geographic scope: This policy applies to all RTMS operations within the United Republic of Tanzania. Data processing occurs on servers hosted in the United States (Render Infrastructure, Ohio) under a Data Processing Agreement that requires compliance with Tanzanian law and international privacy standards.

RTMS is committed to handling all personal data in accordance with the Tanzania Personal Data Protection Act, Cap. 447 (PDPA 2022), the Electronic and Postal Communications Act (EPOCA), and internationally recognised standards including the GDPR framework and the OECD Privacy Guidelines.

Section 03

Legal Basis for Processing

RTMS processes personal data under the following legal bases as defined in the Tanzania Personal Data Protection Act 2022:

3.1 Public Interest & Legal Obligation

Traffic enforcement is a statutory function of the Tanzania Police Force and the Ministry of Works under the Road Traffic Act, Cap 168. RTMS processing of vehicle detection data, violation records, and fine issuance is conducted in furtherance of this legal mandate. No individual consent is required for automated detection of traffic violations on public roads.

3.2 Consent (Citizen Portal)

When a vehicle owner voluntarily creates an account on the RTMS citizen portal, they provide explicit consent to the processing of their personal data for the purpose of viewing their fines, making payments and filing appeals. This consent is freely given, specific, informed and unambiguous as required by PDPA 2022.

3.3 Contractual Necessity

Processing of payment data is necessary for the performance of the payment transaction contract between the vehicle owner and the Tanzania Revenue Authority.

PDPA 2022 Compliance: RTMS is registered as a data controller with the Tanzania Data Protection Commission (or will be registered prior to full deployment). A Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) has been conducted for all automated decision-making processes.
Section 04

Face & Biometric Anonymization

This section describes RTMS's technical implementation of face anonymization, which is a core privacy safeguard and a requirement for compliance with biometric data protection regulations.

4.1 Automatic Face Pixelation

Every vehicle image captured by an RTMS camera undergoes automatic face anonymization before the image is stored, transmitted or displayed. The windshield area of every detected vehicle is processed using a pixelation algorithm that replaces any human facial features with a mosaic pattern, making individual identification impossible. A green "ANON" badge is rendered on the processed image confirming anonymization was applied.

4.2 Pedestrian Masking

Any pedestrians or bystanders captured in the frame of a violation image are automatically masked using object detection. Bystander data is never stored, processed or used for any purpose.

4.3 What is Retained After Anonymization

After anonymization, only the following are retained in the evidence image: the vehicle body, the license plate (for enforcement purposes), and the road/environment context. No facial, biometric or personally identifiable visual data of any individual is stored.

Technical standard: RTMS anonymization conforms to ISO/IEC 29101 Privacy Architecture Framework and is aligned with the approach required by the US DHS ALPR Market Survey Report (2025) and the EU AI Act requirements for automated surveillance systems.
4.4 Override Policy

Face anonymization may only be disabled by a senior system administrator in the context of a specific criminal investigation under a valid court order. Any such override is logged with the officer's identity, timestamp and the authorizing court order reference number.

Section 07

Security Measures

  • Encryption in transit: All data transmitted between cameras, the API server and user browsers uses TLS 1.3 encryption (HTTPS). No plaintext transmission of personal data is permitted.
  • Encryption at rest: The PostgreSQL database is hosted on Render's encrypted infrastructure (AES-256). Backups are encrypted.
  • Password security: All user passwords are hashed using bcrypt with a cost factor of 10. Plaintext passwords are never stored or logged.
  • JWT authentication: All API access requires a signed JSON Web Token with a 30-day expiry. Tokens are invalidated on logout.
  • Role-based access control: Staff access is restricted by role (ADMIN / OFFICER / CASHIER). No single user can access all system functions.
  • Audit logging: All administrative actions, data access events and system changes are logged with user identity, timestamp and IP address.
  • Face anonymization: Applied automatically at the point of capture — see Section 4.
  • No data on personal devices: Officers access the system via a web browser. No enforcement data is stored on personal devices.
Section 08

Your Rights as a Data Subject

Under the Tanzania Personal Data Protection Act 2022, you have the following rights regarding your personal data held by RTMS.

📋
Right to Access
Request a copy of all personal data RTMS holds about you, including violation history and payment records.
✏️
Right to Rectification
Request correction of inaccurate data, such as incorrectly recorded vehicle ownership details.
🗑️
Right to Erasure
Request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal retention requirements (e.g. paid fine records).
🚫
Right to Object
Object to processing of your data for purposes beyond legal enforcement, including any analytics use.
📦
Right to Portability
Receive your personal data in a structured, machine-readable format (JSON/CSV) for transfer to another service.
⚖️
Right to Appeal
Challenge any traffic fine you believe was issued incorrectly through the RTMS appeal system or Traffic Court.

To exercise any of these rights, contact the Data Protection Officer at the address in Section 11. We will respond within 30 days as required by PDPA 2022.

Section 09

Cookies & Online Portal

The RTMS Citizen Portal uses browser localStorage (not cookies) to maintain your login session for 30 days. No tracking cookies, advertising pixels, analytics beacons or third-party scripts are loaded by the RTMS portal. The portal does not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel or any equivalent tracking technology.

Session data stored in localStorage includes: authentication token, user role, display name and session expiry timestamp. This data is cleared automatically when you click "Sign Out" or after 30 days of inactivity.
